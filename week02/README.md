## What is Abstraction?

In simple words, **abstraction** is the process of simplifying complex systems through the use of models or blueprints.
It eases the uses of repetitive tasks and allows easy manipulation of objects. In programming,
abstraction can be seen in the form of *classes* and *functions*. A class is a blueprint for creating objects (or instances)
 that share common proprieties and characteristics.
